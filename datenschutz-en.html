hosting via github pages

this website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc.

when you visit the website, technically necessary connection data is processed. this includes, in particular, the IP address. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages websites for security purposes.

insofar as this processing falls within our responsibility, it is based on our legitimate interest in providing this website securely and reliably pursuant to art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

more information on data processing by GitHub can be found in GitHub's privacy statement (opens in new tab).

adobe fonts

we use Adobe Fonts, a service provided by Adobe, to display our typefaces.

when you visit our website, the required font files are loaded from Adobe's servers. this may transmit information such as the fonts used, the web project ID, the hostname of the page visited, and the IP address.

Adobe states that it receives the IP address to deliver the fonts but does not store it. according to Adobe, Adobe Fonts does not set any cookies when serving web fonts.

insofar as this falls within our responsibility, the integration is based on our legitimate interest in a consistent and visually fitting presentation of our website pursuant to art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

more information on data processing by Adobe Fonts can be found in Adobe's privacy notices (opens in new tab).

töricht live (voting and quizzes at events)

at our events you can take part in votes and quiz rounds with your phone via toericht.eu/play. taking part is voluntary, and you do not need an account.

in doing so we process a nickname of your choice (it does not have to be your real name), your answers and the time taken to answer, the points calculated from them, and the time of your last connection. your nickname and score may be shown on the screen and on other participants' devices during the event.

so that you are recognised after reloading the page or losing your connection, the page stores a random identifier and your nickname in your browser's local storage. we do not set cookies for this. this storage is technically necessary to let you take part (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

for the database and the real-time connection we use Supabase, a service provided by Supabase, Inc. when you open the page, technically necessary connection data, in particular the IP address, is transmitted to Supabase. we upload the images, videos and sounds for our events ourselves; no files are uploaded from your devices.

this processing is based on our legitimate interest in running our events pursuant to art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. we delete the data of a game round no later than 30 days after the event.

more information on data processing by Supabase can be found in Supabase's privacy policy (opens in new tab).

data transfers to third countries

using GitHub Pages, Adobe Fonts and Supabase may result in personal data being processed outside the European Union or the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA.

according to their own statements, the providers base such data transfers on appropriate data protection safeguards, in particular the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

more information on international data transfers can be found in the respective providers' privacy notices.

external links

our website contains links to external services, including our instagram profile. simply visiting our website does not transmit any data to Instagram. only when you follow the link is Instagram's website called up. the respective provider is responsible for any data processing that takes place there.

your rights

subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have the right to request information about your personal data, as well as its correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. you can also object to processing and have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
